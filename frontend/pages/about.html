re.shelf

A marketplace for students to buy and sell second-hand academic resources — textbooks, notes, lab manuals, and more.

How it works

1
Browse or search for what you need
2
Message the seller and agree on a meetup
3
Meet locally and exchange the resource

Why re.shelf?

Academic resources are expensive and often only used for one semester. re.shelf connects students at the same university — or nearby — to make buying and selling easier and more affordable.

Search by university or subject so you find exactly the edition your course requires.
