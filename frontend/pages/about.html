About re.shelf

Students connect.
Resources circulate.

A local marketplace for students to buy, sell, and trade academic resources — textbooks, notes, lab manuals, calculators, stationery, and more — right on your campus.

Our Mission

One local hub for every academic resource you need

Textbooks, lecture notes, lab manuals, calculators, stationery — academic resources are expensive and often used for just one semester. re.shelf connects students at the same university or nearby so these resources keep moving instead of collecting dust.

Because every university has its own required editions, models, and course packs, re.shelf lets you search specifically for what your course needs and find a senior who already has it.

What we stand for

Built on three beliefs

Hyper-local by design

Distance shouldn't be a barrier. re.shelf keeps trades within your campus or neighbourhood so meetups are easy and safe.

Students helping students

We don't take a cut or broker deals. We connect buyer and seller, then step back — payment and handoff are fully between them.

Access for everyone

Cost should never block a student from the tools their course demands. Affordable local trades make that possible.
