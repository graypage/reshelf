FAQ

Is re.shelf free to use?
Yes. Listing and browsing are completely free. re.shelf does not take any commission from sales.
How do I pay for a book?
Payments are handled directly between buyer and seller during the in-person meetup. re.shelf does not process any payments.
What is a "dead listing"?
A dead listing tag is added automatically to listings that have been up for a long time without the seller responding to messages or marking the item as sold. If the seller becomes active again the tag is removed.
Can I list anything?
re.shelf is for academic resources only — textbooks, notes, lab manuals, past papers, and similar study materials.
How do I stay safe when meeting a seller?
Always meet in a public place on or near campus. Tell someone where you are going. Inspect the item before handing over any money.
