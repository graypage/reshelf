Keeping re.shelf simple, safe, and student-friendly.

re.shelf is a student marketplace where students can buy and sell books, stationery, notes, lab manuals, and other academic resources. This page explains the basic rules for using the platform responsibly.

Terms of Use

By using re.shelf, you agree to use the platform respectfully and responsibly. re.shelf is designed to help students connect with other students to buy, sell, or exchange academic items.

Users should provide honest information in their listings and communicate clearly with buyers or sellers.

Marketplace Rules

  • Only list student-related items such as books, notes, stationery, lab manuals, and academic resources.
  • Do not post fake, misleading, offensive, or unrelated listings.
  • Do not spam other users or misuse the messaging system.
  • Agree on the price, condition, and meetup details clearly before exchanging items.

Listings & Content

Sellers are responsible for the accuracy of their listings, including item title, description, price, images, condition, university, and subject details.

re.shelf may remove listings that appear inappropriate, misleading, duplicated, unrelated to students, or against the purpose of the platform.

Safety & Meetups

re.shelf helps students connect, but users are responsible for arranging safe exchanges. Meet in public and familiar places such as campus areas, libraries, cafeterias, or other safe locations.

  • Do not share sensitive personal information unnecessarily.
  • Inspect items before paying.
  • Use clear communication before meeting.
  • Cancel or avoid suspicious interactions.

Privacy

re.shelf may use basic account and listing information to operate the marketplace, display listings, support messaging, and improve the user experience.

Users should avoid posting private information directly inside listing descriptions, images, or public areas of the website.

User Responsibility

re.shelf provides a platform for student-to-student connections. It does not own the listed items and is not directly responsible for the quality, price, condition, or final exchange between users.

Built for student trust

The goal of re.shelf is to make academic resources easier to reuse, resell, and find while keeping the experience simple, respectful, and student-focused.
